How it works

Four steps from emergency to donation.

Create your profile

Donors and patients register, then drop a pin on the map at their exact location. Blood group, availability and health details complete the picture.

Search the map live

Patients search in real time by radius and blood group. Only transfusion-safe, eligible and available donors appear.

AI ranks the matches

Each donor is scored on distance, past responsiveness, reliability and readiness, so the most likely responder is at the top - not just the nearest.

Send the request

Pick donors and send. They get it in their inbox and accept or decline. Contact details are shared only after someone accepts.

Precise geo-location

Exact haversine distance, not city-name guessing. Search any radius up to 100 km.

Learns from every response

The ranking model retrains on real accept and decline outcomes, and explains every score.

Safety rules built in

ABO/Rh compatibility, the 90-day cool-down, age and weight limits are enforced automatically.

Privacy by default

Browsing the map never reveals a phone number or street address. Contact opens on acceptance.

Critical auto-dispatch

Mark a request critical and the engine invites the best donors instantly, no clicking required.

Hospitals included

Blood banks track stock per group, get low-stock flags and raise requests for their patients.
